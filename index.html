HOW TO PLAY 🎮

Cómo jugar

  1. Turn over two cards. — Voltea dos tarjetas.
  2. Read the cards carefully. — Lee las tarjetas.
  3. Find the matching pair. — Encuentra el par correcto.
  4. If they match, keep the pair. — Si coinciden, conserva el par.
  5. If they don't match, try again. — Si no coinciden, inténtalo de nuevo.
  6. The player with the most pairs wins! — Gana quien tenga más pares.

GRAMMAR EXAMPLES 📚

This is her bag.

Este es su bolso.

HER = possession

Her shows that the bag belongs to her.

I like him.

Me gusta él.

HIM = object pronoun

Him receives the action of the verb.

That is mine.

Eso es mío.

MINE = possessive pronoun

Mine shows that something belongs to me.
